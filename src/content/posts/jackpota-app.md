## Tournaments: the social side of the app

Most of the time, spinning a slot is a solo activity. Tournaments change that. A tournament in our app is an event that runs for a set period on a selected group of games. A countdown shows how long it has left, a counter shows how many players have joined, and a leaderboard shows where everyone stands. You're not just playing a game; you're playing it alongside other people who chose the same moment.

That shared moment is what makes tournaments feel different. It's a little like joining a pickup game rather than practicing alone: the activity is the same, but knowing others are there gives it a sense of company. For a lot of players, that sense of being part of something is the appeal, more than where they finish.

Taking part is entirely optional. You can join a tournament, check in on it now and then, or skip it and play at your own pace. Nothing about the rest of the app changes either way.
