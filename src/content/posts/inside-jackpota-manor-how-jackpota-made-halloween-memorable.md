Halloween is one of my favorite times of year at Jackpota, and this October we wanted it to feel like more than a theme. We wanted it to feel like a story you could drop into whenever you liked, find something new, and leave with a smile.

So we built one. Now that the candles are out and the manor doors are closing, here's a look back at how the season came together, and what we noticed about how you enjoyed it.

## A Halloween told in five chapters

Instead of one long event, we split October into a five-part **Halloween Series**, with a new chapter each week:

| Chapter | Title | Dates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Curse Awakes | Oct. 1 to 4 |
| 2 | The Cursed Fortunes | Oct. 5 to 11 |
| 3 | Haunted House of Spins | Oct. 12 to 18 |
| 4 | The Lurking Evil Haunted Reels | Oct. 19 to 25 |
| 5 | Haunted House of Treats & The Escape | Oct. 26 to Nov. 2 |

Each chapter brought its own lineup of Halloween games and events. Read the titles in order and the story unfolds on its own: a curse wakes up, creeps into the house, takes over the reels, and in the end somebody gets out.

What I loved about this structure is how relaxed it felt. Nobody needed to keep up with everything. You could visit for one chapter or several, and each week still felt like opening a fresh page.

## What made it feel special

Looking back, a few things stood out to me about how the season felt:

- **It moved at your pace.** Weekly chapters meant there was always something new to see, without any pressure to catch everything.
- **The characters became familiar faces.** The butler, the doll, the pumpkin-headed host and the Sorceress turned a theme into a cast you could recognize from one video to the next.
- **Everything felt connected.** The logo, the artwork, the videos and the games all shared the same glowing green, deep purple and candlelit gold.

Most of all, it stayed what Jackpota is meant to be: free-to-play entertainment that's there when you want it and easy to step away from when you don't.
